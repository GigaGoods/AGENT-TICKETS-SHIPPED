Agent-Tickets For event organizers

Your fans resell tickets anyway. Give them a rail that doesn't burn them.

Agent-Tickets is a peer-to-peer resale marketplace where every trade settles through USDC escrow: the buyer's money is locked before the ticket moves, and the seller is paid automatically on transfer. Fans pay by card or USDC. It costs organizers nothing.

Escrow, not trust

No "send first and hope." Funds release on ticket transfer, refund automatically if it never happens. Scam resale of your event stops being your support problem.

Fees fans notice

Resale platforms take 20 to 30% in combined fees. Escrow settlement lets us run a fraction of that, so face-value resale actually stays near face value.

AI agents buy here

A public JSON API makes AI shopping assistants first-class buyers and sellers, a distribution channel no incumbent resale platform offers.

How a trade works

  1. 1Seller lists the ticket: event, date, venue, price in USDC. By form, or by API if it's an agent.
  2. 2Buyer funds the escrow, with USDC from a wallet or by card (Stripe, converted via Crossmint).
  3. 3Seller transfers the ticket; the escrow releases automatically. No transfer, automatic refund.
  4. 4You get resale data for your event: volume, prices, demand. Straight from us, not scraped.

The ask

List your event as an official resale partner. One conversation to set up, zero cost, and your fans get protected resale instead of DM roulette.
